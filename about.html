A NOTE FROM THE CURATOR

Curiosity is
the best note.

I built Scent Library to make fragrance discovery a little more approachable. It started as a personal fragrance masterlist and became a small, considered place to explore alternatives.

Connections, not copies.

The collection starts with four popular community comparisons. “Explore alongside” means a scent may be interesting if you enjoy the reference fragrance. It does not promise an identical scent, shared ingredients, performance, or value. Khamrah, for example, is a related gourmand direction rather than an exact replacement for Angels’ Share.

A small library with room to grow.

Search names, brands, families, or notes. Save scents to sample; your shortlist stays in this browser. No account, tracking, or sign-in required. Each card links to a source behind its discovery pairing. Prices and scores are omitted because retailers, batches, and experiences vary.

Made to be explored.

This independent portfolio project uses semantic HTML, CSS, and modular JavaScript. Its checked-in catalog works without API keys or a backend. Brand names belong to their respective owners; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.
