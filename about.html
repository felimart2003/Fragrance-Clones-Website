What Is This?

This is a curated database of designer fragrances paired with their best affordable clones/dupes. Each clone is selected based on the highest-rated "Reminds Me Of" picks on Fragrantica.com — a community of millions of fragrance enthusiasts.

The goal is simple: smell amazing without breaking the bank.

How Clones Are Chosen

For each designer fragrance, the clone listed is the one that the Fragrantica community has voted as the most similar. These aren't random picks — they're crowd-sourced from people who've actually smelled both the original and the dupe.

Popular Clone Houses

  • Armaf — Known for spot-on clones of Creed and niche houses
  • Lattafa — Middle Eastern house with incredible quality-to-price ratio
  • Al Haramain — Premium clones that often rival the originals
  • Zara — Surprisingly good dupes at high-street prices
  • Afnan — Budget-friendly takes on popular designers
  • Rasasi — Arabian perfumery with high-quality ingredients
